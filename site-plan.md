## ARCHITECTURAL SITE PLAN - KEY DETAILS

### Dimensions & Layout

**Property Dimensions:**
- Approximate frontage: 120m (east-west)
- Approximate depth: 135m (north-south)
- 4 acres = 16,187 m²

**Key Distances:**
- Home to barn: 80–100m (livestock downwind)
- Home to workshop: 60–80m (utility node)
- Home to utility core: 40–60m (screened service zone)
- Home to garden: 30–50m (easy access)
- Barn to compost: 15–20m (waste integration)
- Workshop to wood storage: adjacent
- Total drive length: 150–200m

### Building Footprints

**Container Home:**
- 2 × 40 ft containers = 80 ft (24.4 m) × 20 ft (6.1 m) = ~1,500 sq ft
- Alternative: 3 × 40 ft for 2,200+ sq ft
- Roof height: 9.5 ft (2.9m) at eave
- Overhangs: 2m south, 1.5m west, 1m north & east
- Setback from south boundary: 30m minimum

**Workshop / Tool Storage:**
- 40 ft × 30 ft (12m × 9m) = 1,200 sq ft steel frame
- Equipment bay: 30 ft × 20 ft (9m × 6m)
- Height: 14 ft at peak for equipment storage & clearance

**Barn & Animal Structure:**
- Loafing shed: 40 ft × 30 ft (12m × 9m)
- Storage loft above
- Animal pens & coops: modular, moveable
- Height: 12 ft at peak

**Polytunnels:**
- 2 × 30 ft × 100 ft units (9m × 30m each)
- 1 × 20 ft × 60 ft specialty tunnel (6m × 18m)
- Height: 8 ft at peak (full light transmission)

### Utilities & Infrastructure

**Solar Array:**
- 12–18 kW PV capacity
- Ground-mounted or roof-mounted on workshop/barn
- South-facing at 45° tilt for South England latitude
- Clearance from obstacles: minimum 2x array height

**Water Storage Tanks:**
- Potable: 3,000 gal (11 m³) elevated on frame
- Rainwater: 5,000 gal (19 m³) tank
- Reserve/thermal: 2,000 gal (7.5 m³) heated
- Total: 10,000 gal (38 m³) on-site retention

**Septic & Drainage:**
